FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything contractors ask us before getting started.

Will my website be built from scratch or use a generic template?

We build completely custom website designs tailored to your brand identity, business goals and specific industry needs. Every layout is crafted from the ground up to ensure your business stands out from competitors and offers a unique experience to your visitors.

Will my website work well on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes. We develop every site using a mobile-first approach, ensuring your web pages load fast, look sharp and function seamlessly on smartphones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers alike.

How will local customers in my area find my business online?

Our Local SEO setup optimises your website for regional search queries, Google Maps and local directory listings. We optimise your page content, meta tags and location data so customers in your target states and cities can easily discover your services when searching online.

Can I showcase specific projects, case studies or trade-related work?

Absolutely. We design dedicated portfolio pages tailored to your specific industry or trade — complete with high-resolution image galleries, before-and-after sliders and detailed project overviews to demonstrate your expertise to potential clients.

How do custom forms help convert website visitors into leads?

We craft strategic, user-friendly forms designed to reduce friction and encourage visitors to take action. Whether it is requesting a quote, booking an inspection or asking a question, our high-conversion forms capture quality leads directly from your site.

How does the instant chat feature work for new website inquiries?

When a visitor submits an inquiry or initiates a live chat, our automated response integration instantly sends an SMS or chat reply to engage them within seconds. This ensures you never miss a lead, even outside normal business hours.

How can we filter out unqualified leads before spending time on them?

Smart Job Qualification uses interactive multi-step forms and automated logic to ask prospective clients key questions about their budget, timeline and scope of work before they submit a request. This saves your team time by screening leads automatically.

Can clients schedule appointments or consultations directly on the site?

Yes. We connect your website to your preferred calendar software (such as Google Calendar, Calendly or Outlook). Clients can view your real-time availability and book an appointment or consultation directly without back-and-forth emails.

What happens if a client submits an urgent or off-hours service request?

Our emergency escalation feature detects urgent keywords or high-priority selections on your contact forms and immediately routes SMS notifications or phone alerts to on-call team members, ensuring rapid response for critical issues.
